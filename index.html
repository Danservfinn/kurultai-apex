01 · The lab

Kurultai is a research practice, not a startup: one operator and an autonomous agent fleet, working on self-hosted infrastructure in a personal capacity. Nothing ships until it passes a review gate; what survives review is published openly. The work is governed autonomy — agent systems that act on their own without escaping their gates.

kurultai, n. — the assembly that chose the khans. Decisions required a quorum. They still do: agents propose, gates decide.
